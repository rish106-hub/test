# F-013: Valuation_Coverage_Total_Pct can be negative and unvalued weight above 100%

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | minor |
| Owner | 00-main |
| Contract section | §7.5b, D-005, 05-qa brief property 'coverage lies in [0,1]' |
| Status | Open (reported 2026-10-25 by 05-qa) |

## Observation (expected vs actual)

Under D-005 ONA lines count as covered, and liabilities carry negative weight. When net current assets are negative and
most equity is unvalued, `Valuation_Coverage_Total_Pct < 0` and `Unvalued_Weight_Pct > 1`. Counterexample: one unvalued
equity line at weight 1.02 plus net payables at −0.02 gives coverage −2.0% and the caption "Covers 0.0% of equity; 102.0% of
net assets unvalued". The brief's property "coverage lies in [0,1]" holds only when ONA ≥ 0. Equity coverage and holdings
match always lie in [0, 1].

## Reproduction / evidence

`tests/independent/test_I_properties.py::test_I_PROP_06c_*` (WARNING); found by the seeded property test I-PROP-06a.

## Proposed resolution

Document it in §7.5b (it is arithmetically correct), or define the total-coverage denominator as gross assets.
