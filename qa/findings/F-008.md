# F-008: Blend weight sum tolerance is unspecified

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | minor |
| Owner | 00-main |
| Contract section | §3.8, T-BNK-08 |
| Status | Open (reported 2026-10-25 by 05-qa) |

## Observation (expected vs actual)

`Σ w_m = 1 (validated)`. T-BNK-08 shows that 0.9 fails, but no tolerance is given. Weights typed as thirds (0.3333 × 3) or read back from Excel can miss 1 by 1e-4 or 1e-16. The clean-room uses 1e-9.

## Reproduction / evidence

`tests/independent/cleanroom/valuation.py::BLEND_WEIGHT_TOL`.

## Proposed resolution

State a tolerance (e.g. 1e-9, matching §8's probabilities) in §3.8.
