# F-011: EXECUTIVE_DASHBOARD lacks unvalued count/weight and the NAV-date warning required by W-12

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | major |
| Owner | 00-main (build/main_sheets.py::build_dashboard) |
| Contract section | W-12, §7.5b ('must appear on EXECUTIVE_DASHBOARD') |
| Status | Open (reported 2026-10-25 by 05-qa) |

## Observation (expected vs actual)

`build_dashboard` links Coverage_Caption, the three statuses, the dates, Holdings_Age_Days, Max_Financials_Age_Days and
Stale_Valued_Weight_Pct. It does **not** link `Unvalued_Count`, `Unvalued_Weight_Pct` or `NAV_Date_Gap_Days`. W-12 requires
the "unvalued count/weight and holdings-date/price-date warnings for every plan". The unvalued weight appears only inside the
caption text, and the count does not appear at all. W-12 will FAIL on the first integrated build that has tbl_NAVSummary.

## Reproduction / evidence

Static review of `build/main_sheets.py` (fields list). W-12 (`tests/workbook/test_W_values.py`) requires links to Unvalued_Count, Unvalued_Weight_Pct and NAV_Date_Gap_Days. Its logic passes on the QA self-test workbook (W-SELF-02).

## Proposed resolution

Add the three fields to the dashboard's field list, with labels.
