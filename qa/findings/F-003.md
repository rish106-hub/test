# F-003: 'Shock inputs read in exactly one place' conflicts with Control_Warning and Controls_At_Default

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | minor |
| Owner | 00-main |
| Contract section | §6.1 rule 2, D-024, W-17 vs §6.2 and §6.3 |
| Status | Open (reported 2026-10-25 by 05-qa) |

## Observation (expected vs actual)

§6.1 rule 2, D-024 and W-17 say the three shock inputs are read **only** in `tbl_PriceControls[Effective_Shock_Pct]`.
But §6.2 requires `Control_Warning` to flag "any of the three shocks ≠ 0" when the mode is not Shock. It cannot be derived
from `Effective_Shock_Pct`, because shocks can cancel: (1 + 0.10)(1 − 1/11) − 1 = 0. §6.3's `Controls_At_Default` must also
read every input. A literal W-17 would fail a correct 03-nav build.

## Reproduction / evidence

`tests/workbook/test_W_static.py::test_W_17_shocks_are_read_once` tolerates `Control_Warning` and boolean default checks, and lists them in its evidence.

## Proposed resolution

Amend D-024 / W-17 to say: shock inputs feed *prices* only through Effective_Shock_Pct; Control_Warning and Controls_At_Default may read them.
