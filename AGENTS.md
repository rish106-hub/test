# AGENTS.md

This repository is worked on by several coding agents in parallel. The rules are tool-neutral and live in two files:

1. **`tracker.md`**: current state, who owns what, open risks, and how to take over or relaunch a worker. Read it first.
2. **`CLAUDE.md`**: the shared engineering rules and non-negotiables. They bind every agent, whatever its vendor.

Then read your brief in `spec/worker_briefs/<id>.md`. If no one assigned you a role, you are 00-main (integration); see `tracker.md` §1.
