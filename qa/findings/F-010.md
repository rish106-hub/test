# F-010: Threshold descriptions contradict the contract's reference dates

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Severity | minor |
| Owner | 00-main |
| Contract section | data/reference/global_inputs.csv vs §7.5c, §7.1 |
| Status | Open (reported 2026-10-25 by 05-qa) |

## Observation (expected vs actual)

* `in_Holdings_Stale_Days`: "Holdings older than this **vs in_Price_Date**", but §7.5c measures `Valuation_Date − Portfolio_Date`.
* `in_Price_Stale_Days`: "Price observations older than this vs in_Price_Date", but §7.5c also uses it for `NAV_Date_Gap_Days`
  measured to `Valuation_Date`.
* §7.1 names a `NAV_Date_Warning`. tbl_NAVSummary has `NAV_Date_Gap_Days` and no NAV_Date_Warning column.
These are identical while `in_Price_Date = in_Valuation_Date`, and they diverge as soon as the two dates differ.

## Reproduction / evidence

Text comparison of global_inputs.csv, contract §7.5c and schema.NAV_SUMMARY.

## Proposed resolution

Align the descriptions with §7.5c. Rename §7.1's NAV_Date_Warning to NAV_Date_Gap_Days, or add the column.
